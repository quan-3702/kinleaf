Kinleaf – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Kinleaf works entirely on your device. The names, dates, notes and photos you enter are stored locally on your PC and are never uploaded to any server.

Backup files, pictures and GEDCOM files are created on your PC and saved only where you choose.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
